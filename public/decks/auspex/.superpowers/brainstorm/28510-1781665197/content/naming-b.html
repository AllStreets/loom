Name the Planetary Commons

A watching instrument that helps the world. The name should hold both halves: awareness and care — never surveillance. Click the one that feels like it could be on the lips of millions. Your own ideas welcome too.

VIGIL
A watch kept out of care. To "keep vigil" is to stay awake over someone so harm doesn't reach them unseen — exactly what this does for the planet.
Watchful · Humane · Real word everyone knows
AUSPEX
The ancient watcher who read the world's signs to forewarn the city. Literally an early-warning system, two thousand years old. Sits beside Aegis, Cortex, Specter in your lineage.
Mythic · Unique · Ownable · "Reads the omens"
BEACON
A light raised so everyone can see the danger — and the way through. Warm, hopeful, instantly understood in every language. The most "for the world" of the four.
Hopeful · Accessible · Warning + guidance
POLARIS
The one fixed star the whole world navigates by. A shared point of truth and orientation when everything else is moving. Elevated, calm, planetary.
Shared · Orienting · A commons everyone looks to

Runners-up if none land: Argus (the hundred-eyed all-seer — but leans surveillance), Pangaea (one world), Concordia (harmony of all), Aether (the living medium of the heavens). Tell me which moves you — or throw a word of your own.
