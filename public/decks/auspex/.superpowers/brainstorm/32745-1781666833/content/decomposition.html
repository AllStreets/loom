Does v1 lead with Disasters — or a different first sense?

A

Disasters first  (recommended)

USGS quakes + GDACS. Cleanest free data, most visceral, clearest life-saving value. Proves the whole instrument fast.

B

Conflict & unrest first

Highest stakes, but messier data and licensing (ACLED/GDELT). Heavier to do honestly.

C

Supply chains first

Leans on your Flexport lineage (AIS/ports). Strong, but less universally felt than disasters.

D

Breadth over depth

All domains, shallow. Faster to look impressive — but won't hit the "worthy of the world" bar. I'd advise against it.
